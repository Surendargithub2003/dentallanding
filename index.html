FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with us.

Who owns the code? +

You do. Once milestones are completed and paid for, you own 100% of the intellectual property, design assets, and source code. We hand over the keys and help host it in your account.

How do we communicate during development? +

You will talk directly with the engineers building your product via a dedicated Slack channel. We hold weekly demo calls, use Loom for async updates, and keep a transparent Jira/Linear board.

Do you offer post-launch support? +

Yes. Every project includes 30 days of free bug-fixing support. After that, we offer monthly maintenance retainers to handle system updates, server management, and small iterations.

What is your typical project timeline? +

Most custom web portals and MVP mobile apps take between 6 to 12 weeks from discovery to launch. We will provide an exact timeline breakdown during the discovery stage.

Tell us what you're building. We'll respond with next steps, not a sales pitch.
